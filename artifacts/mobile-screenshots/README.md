# Mahaly mobile screen gallery

Captured from the verified Expo web export across 320 × 568, 390 × 844,
and 430 × 932 mobile viewports.

Public screens use live Mahaly catalog data. Screens that require a customer
session are captured at their signed-out boundary; authenticated order,
address, wishlist, profile, and review states require a dedicated test account
with eligible purchase data.

The numbered filenames follow the customer journey from splash and onboarding
through browsing, checkout, account, brand, reviews, and dark appearance.

`09b-product-filters.png` records the live catalog filter sheet, including
availability, brands, product types, price bands, colors, and sizes.
